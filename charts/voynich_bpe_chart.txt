Voynich-BPE experiment: BPE tokenizers trained on the Voynich EVA transliteration (ZL3b) vs Latin, English, Italian, and shuffled controls (~225KB each). Left: Voynich (gold) compresses BETTER than every natural language, while char-shuffled Voynich (red dashed) collapses — Voynichese is highly structured, not gibberish, but super-regular. Right: its Zipf curve nests among the real languages. Word-shuffled control is exactly identical to Voynich (hidden under the gold line) — BPE is blind to word order, where the real weirdness lives.
