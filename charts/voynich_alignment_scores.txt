Voynich phase 5 — the honest null. Seed-free Gromov-Wasserstein alignment of Voynich embeddings to Latin and Italian, calibrated both ways: Latin→Italian (two real related languages) should be the ceiling, alignments to word-shuffled corpora the floor. Result: the ceiling sits INSIDE the floor band (ρ≈0.16 everywhere) — at ~35K tokens, unsupervised alignment has zero discriminative power; GW aligns generic point-cloud geometry, not meaning. Voynich→Latin's 0.171 is noise, and no "word translations" are claimable. The nomenclator hypothesis rests on the section-topology evidence instead. 🜂
