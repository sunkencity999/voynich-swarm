## Design intent

Convert WP4's post-hoc folio-opening discovery into preregistered evidence, and scope it:
P-A (is the opening enrichment real at cluster level in Currier-A folios?), P-B (is
Currier-B a clean negative control — i.e., is this an "A-language" phenomenon?), P-H (is it
just a heading/incipit geometric confound?), plus scale tests (recto/verso, quire-opening,
section-opening) and internal-geometry tests (does the corpus line-final step persist inside
the opening paragraph?).

## Master verdict

**MIXED (revised-scope; heading confound disfavored but not fully discharged).** Not
SUPPORTED (P-B failed as stated; P-H letter failed); not KILLED (both preregistered kill
conditions explicitly unmet). The certified statement of what the effect IS:

- A real, **folio-cluster-certified** enrichment of or- in the first paragraph of a page —
  pooled (WP4) and within Currier A (T1a: +.0137 ZL, p=.002, boot CI [+.0026,+.0238];
  +.0127 IT, p=.003, boot CI [+.0031,+.0212]).
- **Robust to geometric heading-likeness** (T2a certified under both frozen annotation
  variants, p=.002/.001, boot CIs exclude 0); heading-like paragraphs are themselves
  or--DEPLETED (T2b), the opposite of the confound's requirement.
- A **page-level** phenomenon: present on recto and verso alike (T3a), no quire-opening
  signal (T3b), no certifiable section-opening component (T3c underpowered).
- **Section-modulated:** positive in S/P/H, **negative in balneological** (−.002/−.003) —
  not a Currier-language split.
- NOT certified beyond the opening paragraph's first line at cluster level (T2c:
  permutation-strong p=.001/.002, but boot CI includes 0 both translits — exactly 50/1000
  and 82/1000 resamples ≤ 0).
- NOT shown to retain the line-final step inside the opening paragraph (T4a fail ZL /
  non-certifiable IT): the opening zone is HEAD-anchored (first line hottest), unlike the
  corpus-wide line-final step — two distinct positional signatures.
