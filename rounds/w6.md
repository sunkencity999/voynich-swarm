## Design intent: the instrument WP9 said we needed

WP9's semantics round ended with a binding method constraint: dilation-based masking
of a binarized ink mask cannot simultaneously pass text-leakage gates and leave
annotatable drawing substrate (median masked fraction .777; 22/171 pages >90%
masked). Revival of U1 — the blind semantic annotation that could discharge the
one-line-heading question (ledger B1) — requires **stroke-level text/drawing
separation**. W6 was that instrument round, and only that: verdict space
{INSTRUMENT-CERTIFIED, INSTRUMENT-FAILED, GATE-INFRA-FAILED}, all three publishable;
**no semantic claim of any kind permitted**.

The instrument: a classical-CV stroke-morphology segmenter over the WP8/WP9 frozen
front end, classifying connected components into TEXT vs DRAWING and inpainting the
text away — hardened during tuning (on TUNE pages only) into a hybrid with local VL
arbitration for ambiguous star/script components (a disclosed deviation; the G3
verifier and all G1/G2 probes are OpenAI-family and independent of the segmenter).
Three preregistered gates, bars frozen before any segmenter code: **G1** text-removal
completeness with an explicit probe-power precondition (≥54/60 positive-control
patches probed "yes" before leakage may be scored — built to be strictly stronger
than WP9's thin P1), **G2** substrate preservation (median footprint ≤0.40, zero
pages >0.90, ≤3/30 >0.60; ≥90% page-level drawing retention), **G3** groundedness
(≥27/30 annotator-definite cells verified by a third family — WP9's bar, unchanged).

Round 0's G3 diagnosis was specific: removal dilation carried no drawing guard and
**shaved star arms** where text sits close to drawing ink (f112r/f112v star-count
cells), plus annotator judgment errors on intact features. The ONE allowed revision
(v1.9) added exactly that guard — `DRAW_GUARD_PRIMARY`, preregistered in BUILD_LOG
before round 1, verified on TUNE only. It was not enough: round 1's G3 failed harder
(21/30), with the same star pages failing the same way, and round-1 G1 could not even
field its probe — 7 of the 11 prompt-v1 "no" positive-control patches contain **no
text at all**. The positive-control construction ("patch centers are centroids of
removed text components, therefore contain text") was violated by the segmenter
itself: it had removed drawing/edge ink it misclassified as text, planting positive
controls on textless spots. Honest ceiling 53/60 < bar 54 — no prompt could pass.

This is a result about THIS segmenter, not about the manuscript, and not a
feasibility ceiling for the approach class. No semantic claim was made. U1/B1 remain
gated on a certified instrument that does not yet exist.

## The salvage ruling: what a failed instrument still certifies

The builder's summary called the G2 result "the stroke-level approach solves the
substrate-preservation problem." Smaug recomputed and confirmed the numbers — then
ruled **"SOLVED" is too big a word for what was certified**: G2b's page-level "any
drawing left?" bar provably passes while star arms are being shaved, and a low
footprint fraction does not mean removal was well-targeted. What IS certified, as an
adversary-granted lead in the adversary's own binding words:

> **CERTIFIED LEAD (W6-A):** On 38 distinct held-out pages over two seeded rounds,
> the W6 stroke-level segmenter class held removal footprint to median ≈.15 (max
> .43, zero pages >0.60) with 60/60 page-level drawing retention under an
> independent-family probe — versus WP9 dilation-masking's median .777 with 22/171
> pages >90% masked. Substrate *survival* is solved for this approach class;
> substrate *fidelity* (fine drawing detail near text) and removal *targeting*
> (drawing ink misclassified as text) are explicitly NOT solved and are the proven
> failure modes.

## Binding prerequisites for any successor instrument round

Entered into the ledger as U1-revival stage-1 requirements (W6-A, verbatim):

1. **Decoupled positive controls** — G1 probe-power patches drawn from text locations
   verified independently of the segmenter under test, never from the segmenter's
   own removed-component centroids.
2. **A drawing-fidelity gate stronger than page-level retention** — a preregistered
   bar on drawing-detail survival (e.g. star-count preservation, or drawing-ink IoU
   original-vs-removed), since G2b provably passes while star arms are shaved.
3. **Annotator-noise budget for G3** — adjudication, a pre-measured annotator error
   rate with the bar set net of it, or multi-annotator: 3+ of 9 round-1 failures
   were annotator errors on intact features, which a 27/30 bar cannot absorb at
   pool ≈65.
4. **Power-precondition margin** — probe-power bars passed with margin ≥2 or
   replicated on a second draw before leakage is scored (round 0's knife-edge is the
   cautionary precedent).
5. **Family-separation disclosure** — any VL assist inside the segmenter declared at
   freeze with its family relationship to the G3 annotator.

## What this means

The campaign built the instrument WP9 demanded, gated it honestly, and it failed —
twice, terminally, with the failure modes now localized and specific: drawing ink
misclassified as removable text, fine drawing detail degraded near text, and
annotator noise a 27/30 bar cannot absorb. The adversary then attacked the negative
verdict as hard as any positive one, found the round-0 pass had been sampling luck,
downgraded the only leakage evidence the round produced, and granted exactly one
scoped lead: substrate survival — the thing WP9's masking catastrophically could not
do — is demonstrated for this approach class. A failed instrument, certified fairly,
with the path for its successor written into law. The one-line-heading question (B1)
stays open; U1 stays gated.
