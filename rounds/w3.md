## What the adversary overturned (the part that matters)

The builder's frozen wording published the causal claim *"the self-citation family as
implemented cannot be tuned to the corpus's unigram/word-structure surface"*, explained
by a claimed structural tension: the TTR window (needs mutation to mint types) and the
top-100-mass window (needs exact copies to concentrate mass) "never overlap".

**False.** Using the builder's **own committed generator code, unchanged**, Smaug found
**8 joint (both-translit) full four-bar certification passes off the frozen grid** at
τ ∈ {6000, 12000, 16000} (5 draws/point, adversary seeds) — e.g. (τ=12000,
p_exact=0.675, p2=0.2, p_cont=0): ZL TTR 0.173, top-100 mass 0.378; IT 0.186, 0.355.
The constraint windows overlap for τ ≳ 6000; the builder's D1 diagnostic scanned the
wrong p_exact range at large τ (the passing corridor drifts down in p_exact as τ grows)
and inferred structural impossibility from a corner of the space. Honest error, wrong
inference — ruled "honest inferential failure, not sandbagging" (the frozen grid spans
the published T&S recency scales; the bars are the identical bars the memoryless family
passed, and the adversary's own result proves they are passable).

**Mechanism note that set up W3b:** at τ ≥ 6000 over a ~38K-token history the
exp(−d/τ) recency kernel is nearly flat — source selection is approximately uniform
over history, i.e. copy probability proportional to current type frequency. The
certified regime is a frequency-preferential (Yule–Simon-like) regime, which the
builder had proposed as a *future redesign* without noticing their own code already
contained it.
