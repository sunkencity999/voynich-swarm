## Design intent

Every WP-series round bounded what the *or-* system is NOT. W2 is the campaign's first
potential **evidence-FOR** instrument. Motivation: Greshko's "Naibbe cipher"
(Cryptologia 2025) is a period-feasible table+dice cipher that reproduces Voynich-like
statistics but — per the literature-sweep characterization — leaks plaintext
digram/trigram residue into the ciphertext stream, while memoryless generators
(Rugg-style grille, Stolfi-style core-mantle sampling) draw each word independently and
leak none. The paper's own statistic was not available (paywalled); the round's residue
measures are derived from first principles (character-digram-class mutual information,
conditioned to remove positional-slot structure) and no claim depends on the paper's
internal numbers.

Three preregistered prongs against two null families (structure-preserving shuffles of
the real corpus; certified memoryless generators tuned only on unigram/word-structure
statistics):

- **P1 — boundary residue:** MI between word-final and word-initial character classes
  across adjacent-word junctions (statistic S_b).
- **P2 — within-word positional residue:** digram MI conditioned on (word-length,
  position) cells, i.e. beyond the known slot grammar (statistic S_w).
- **P3 — generator separation:** do generators certified to match the manuscript on
  four unigram/word bars (H1 ±0.10 bits, mean length ±0.50, TTR ±20% rel, top-100 mass
  ±20% rel) reproduce the observed S_b and raw within-word MI S_w_raw?

**Frozen honest-scope wording (binding):** a pass supports "sequential digram-class
structure inconsistent with memoryless independent-draw generation as implemented
here" — it does NOT certify "cipher" or "language". Memory-bearing
(self-citation-class) generators are outside this round's null family.

## Master verdict: **P1 PASS · P2 PASS · P3 SUPPORTED (all cells) · master SUPPORTED — the campaign's first positive certified constraint (adversary: CONFIRMED-WITH-AMENDMENTS, AM-W2-1..4)**

Voynichese carries sequential digram-class structure — within words beyond the
positional slot grammar, AND across word boundaries — that structure-preserving shuffles
destroy and certified memoryless generators cannot reproduce. Every primary tail is
exact 1/1001 with zero nulls at or above the observation; margins are wide, not
knife-edge (the preregistered knife-edge menu never triggered).

## Adversary round (W2-A) — the audit that drew blood without overturning

Ruling: **CONFIRMED-WITH-AMENDMENTS.** Every headline number reproduced by an
independent code path to ~1e-13; checkpoint replay from the declared per-draw
SeedSequence mapping was bit-identical (verifying the crash-resume claim from first
principles); disjoint-seed nulls (2000 draws on P1, 500 independent-implementation
draws on P2) gave n_ge = 0 everywhere; P3 re-run at adversary seeds (K = 500 fresh
corpora per cell) separates in all 8 cell × transliteration units, robust up to a
λ = 0.95 probe (the strongest admissible bag-of-attested-words). Fabrication screen
clean: commit ordering monotone, no outcome-bearing number in any pre-run commit.

**The kill vector that drew blood — the comma artifact (→ AM-W2-1):** the inherited
tokenizer splits words on IVTFF *uncertain-space* commas as well as hard spaces. In ZL,
**8.02% of boundary junctions are commas**, and they are MI-hot (comma-only
S_b = 0.532 bits) — inflating ZL's pooled boundary figure by ~14%. But the effect
survives excision: hard-space-only ZL S_b = **0.1712**, still ~12× its own null max
(p = 1/1001, n_ge = 0) — and IT contains zero comma junctions at source yet
independently shows S_b = 0.178. The contamination inflates the number; it does not
create the effect. Per AM-W2-1 the hard-space-only figure travels with any published
boundary number, permanently.

Other kill vectors attempted and dead: line-position pooling (induced-MI upper bound
0.027 bits, an order of magnitude below observed; position-stratified S_b *rises* to
0.280/0.250; a folio-and-position-preserving null still gives n_ge = 0), Currier/
section/folio mixing (folio-stratified cell null: mixing contributes only ~0.035 bits
of S_w; n_ge = 0/1000), generators-tuned-to-lose (the opposite verified — the frozen
smallest-λ rule picks the most recombination-heavy certifier, and the λ = 0.95 probe
still separates), and transliteration coincidence (IT is comma-free and concordant).

**Cell-count reconciliation:** DESIGN defines 4 P3 cells (generator × statistic), each
required to separate in both transliterations = 8 cell × translit units; 4/4 and 8/8
describe the same complete separation (cosmetic wording defect only).

**Binding amendments (campaign law):**

- **AM-W2-1 — junction-type disclosure:** adjacency claims must classify junction types
  at freeze; published boundary statistics carry the hard-junction-only figure alongside
  the pooled one.
- **AM-W2-2 — certification draw floor:** generator-certification grids use ≥5 draws
  per grid point (single-draw λ selection is a seed lottery at bar boundaries; the
  adversary's regrid flipped one non-outcome-bearing cell, A1.ZL 0.70 vs 0.75).
- **AM-W2-3 — computed ceilings:** feasibility/ceiling notes quoted at freeze must be
  computed from frozen inputs by committed code, never asserted from theory (DESIGN's
  claim that all bars are achievable at λ = 1.0 was wrong — with-replacement resampling
  loses hapaxes and undershoots the TTR floor; non-binding here since interior grid
  points certified, and the builder disclosed it honestly).
- **AM-W2-4 — λ-asymmetry caveat travels:** any citation of W2's P3 must state that the
  S_w_raw separation depends on the TTR bar bounding λ < 1; the bag-of-attested-words
  alternative is excluded by S_b/P1, which is the λ-robust separator.

## What this means (exact certified scope, nothing more)

Two facts enter the ledger: the manuscript's character stream carries sequential
digram-class structure at word boundaries and within words beyond the slot grammar
(Tier-A A10), and the manuscript is not the output of the certified memoryless
generator family — Rugg-style grille or Stolfi-style core-mantle sampling tuned to
match its unigram/word statistics (Tier-A A11). This is an exclusion of a generator
class, not a reading: it does not certify cipher, does not certify language, and says
nothing yet about memory-bearing generators (Timm-style self-citation), which are the
natural W3 target.

**NOT_PREREG leads to record:** the transcriber's uncertain spaces concentrate at
high-digram-cohesion junctions (comma-only S_b ≈ 0.53 bits) — Voynichese "word"
segmentation may be soft, morpheme-boundary-like, rather than crisp (a
soft-word-segmentation round candidate); position-conditioning strengthens the boundary
signal (stratified S_b 0.28 vs pooled 0.20), so a position-conditioned boundary prong
would be more powerful; and folio mixing's share of within-word MI is now quantified
(≈ 0.035 bits).
